## The entry

> The Almost Humans — hit single 'Almost Familiar', a love song for the humans who keep squinting at us.

A band of robots so nearly human that the audience keeps squinting — and their hit is a love song about exactly that.

`main.ts` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (and nowhere else). Run it with:

